# Pondside v1.0.0

*Monday 12 October 2026*

A cozy, local-first Windows companion for hard days, starring **Peepers** — a
small watercolour frog who holds your worries, times your walks, breathes with
you, and keeps a gentle weekly review.

He is a presence, not a productivity tool. He celebrates that you showed up,
not what you got done.

---

## What's inside

- **Peepers, alive** — he blinks every few seconds, breathes slowly from
  planted feet, and sleeps with his eyes shut when he naps.
- **One-tap check-ins** — Rough / Okayish / Good. Two seconds, no follow-up
  questions.
- **Timers that do the math** — start something, live your life, stop it. The
  duration is computed for you, then a soft *"did it help?"*.
- **Breathe with me** — sixty seconds: four in, six out, six slow breaths.
  Ends with *"there you are."*
- **The worry jar** — give Peepers what's heavy, or just tap *"it's heavy"*
  when words won't come. The jar holds a count, not a pile. Nothing in there
  expires, and it waits for you — not the other way around.
- **Journal** — short notes. One line is a complete entry.
- **Week** — a gentle look back: what helped first, soft numbers after.
- **Exports you own** — a weekly one-page PDF for therapy or your own shelf,
  and a full backup as JSON + CSV.
- **Tray and quiet mode** — Peepers naps while you work. Quiet mode is always
  on: there are no notifications, ever.

## Install

Download **Pondside-Setup-1.0.0.exe** below and run it. It installs just for
you, under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Pondside` — **no administrator prompt**.
Uninstall any time from the Start Menu.

Then run him with `C:\Pondside\run_pondside.bat`, or from the Start Menu.

## Your data

Everything lives in one SQLite file beside the app, on your machine. No
account, no cloud, no telemetry, no analytics. Exports happen only when you
ask for them.

Uninstalling **never deletes your data** — your check-ins, notes, worries,
config and exports are left exactly where they are.

## The promises

- **No water is ever shown.** The pond is behind the log; you never have to
  look at it.
- **No streaks, no scores, no red numbers, no "you should".**
- **Nothing that could feel like surveillance.** If it would make a hard day
  harder, it isn't in here.

## Notes

- Windows only for now. Built and tested on Windows 11.
- The blink is Peepers closing his own eyes; the sleep pose is a deeper
  version of the same, so he never changes shape when he nods off.
- If an art file is ever missing, he falls back to the painted glade and keeps
  working — nothing breaks.
